BRIEF:

Casestudy: Using AI for full lifecycle development by building a procedurally generated environment modelled of The Grampians in Unreal


DESCRIPTION:

A casestudy on using AI Development for full lifecycle project development in the Unreal Engine. The demonstratable project being to generate a high fidelity Tech Demo of a procedurally generated landscape modelled off the Grampians in Western Victoria Victoria


EXPECTED BENEFITS:

Referencable documentation and use-cases for full lifecycle project development work using frontier AI models and workflows


OVERVIEW:

Build a high fidelity procedurally generated scene environment in the Unreal Engine modelled off the Grampians in Western Victoria using AI development processess exclusively 


OVERALL GOALS:

- A project vehicle for me to upskill from Unity to the Unreal Engine
- Apply the AI development knowledge I have gained from previous projects into the Unreal platform
- Use a clearly defined and well scoped project deliverable to guide actions and choices
- Investigate and demonstrate the application of expansive research into the project’s topic and it’s development process
- Demonstrate how to use AI to move through the development cycles: ideation, research, prototyping, implementation and refinement for a stated deliverable. 
- Develop tools, frameworks and speficifiations for each phase, especially review and validation 


AI USAGE TENANTS:
- this project will leverage AI to assist in the planning and researching of the project as well as in orchestrating the development of the project's code and assets.
- At all times, the AI will confer with me over ambiguoity and assumptions for tasks and processess
- All AI developed work will be generated in a way as to be easily readable/parsable and editable by myself or explained in simple terms if knowledge gaps exist
- AI will independently fact check and review work it's created on the assumption that it may be incorrect or have drifted from the intended purpose
- All planning done via an AI agent will adhere to the Matt-Pocock skills templates for wayfinding and grilling
- All AI planning will be done with higher tier models such as Opus or Fable with high reasoning while development, research and execution tasks will be executed by lesser models as required
- CI planning will be important to reduce bottlenecks created by simultaneous agents requesting to work in the editor or with assets/scripts at the same time
- GITNexus will be used to maintain a memory map of the project that orchestrators and planning sessions can refer to for exsiting functionality and utilities that can be used or will be impacted by development
- All tasks that produce work will be segemneted and broken down so that they are single purpose in design and intent 
- Critical thinking should be applied to all problems or hurdles as there are often more than one solution to a given task. However no solutions should break the rules
