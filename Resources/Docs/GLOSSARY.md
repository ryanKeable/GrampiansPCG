# Glossary

**Milestone**
A numbered phase of the project from the brief (M01, M02, …). On the Kanban board it is an epic card.

**Map**
A milestone's epic card once it has been charted with wayfinder: it holds the destination, notes, decisions so far, and what is not yet specified.

**Card**
A child ticket of a milestone, one single-purpose piece of work on the Kanban board.

**Test & pass requirements**
The numbered criteria a card is reviewed against: what to check, how to check it, and the expected result. Written before the card is built.

**Journal**
The author's write-up of the work being done, kept to meet the case-study requirements. Separate from the project's deliverable.
